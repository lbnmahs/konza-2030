# Acceptable use

The code is licensed under Apache-2.0 (see `LICENSE`). The licence sets what you may legally do; this page asks how you use this particular project, because it acts for residents in public services and speaks with an AI voice.

## Please do

- Use it to study, teach, test and improve how AI assistants for public services can be made safe: rules that decide instead of the model, read-backs before anything irreversible, human review of every adverse decision, independent checking.
- Use synthetic people, numbers and addresses while you build and test, and name your agencies so none is mistaken for a real body.
- Say clearly who runs your instance and that it is not affiliated with any government body unless it is; keep the marker on every text message (the SMS sender refuses texts without it).
- Keep the assistant saying it is an AI in its first message.

## Please do not

- Run it as a public service without the authority, legal basis and impact assessment that requires.
- Impersonate any real government, agency, official or brand: no real crests, logos, colours, domains, phone numbers or names presented as theirs.
- Load real people's personal data, identity numbers, health or family details into it.
- Clone or imitate a real person's voice without that person's written consent, or an official's voice at all.
- Use it to collect payments, credentials or codes from anyone.
- Point its phone or SMS tools at anyone who has not agreed to receive them, or remove the recipient allowlists and daily budgets.

## If you build on it

Run your own impact assessment before any real use (the template and a filled example are in `docs/konza/impact-assessment.md`), follow your data protection law, and keep a human in charge of every decision that affects someone.
